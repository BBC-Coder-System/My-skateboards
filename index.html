Music source
For YouTube Music, Spotify and other apps (iPhone doesn't let any app read their sound). The lights keep a steady beat on their own; Auto-listen finds it for you, or tap it in.
Auto-listen uses the microphone only to find the beat (it listens for 8 s at a time). The lights never react to single sounds, so wind and wheel noise don't cause random flashes. It re-locks by itself when the song changes.
Tapping: 8 or more taps along with the beat. Always overrides Auto-listen.

Strip setup

      
Tell the controller how many LEDs your strip has. If the number is wrong, moving effects stop early, stretch, or jump. The controller stores this, so you only need to do it once.

      

        
        
        
      

      

        
        
        
        
      

      

        
        
      

      
A 2 m strip usually has 60 (30/m), 120 (60/m) or 288 (144/m) LEDs. Count the LEDs in 10 cm and multiply by 20. Test sends a single red tail down the strip: with the right count it runs smoothly from one end to the other.
